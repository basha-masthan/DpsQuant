Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

Use “Explore Courses” → pick a course → click Enroll. After login, it appears on your dashboard with progress tracking.

Will I get placement assistance?

Yes. You’ll get company-wise practice sets, resume reviews, and mock interviews with feedback.

Are classes live or recorded?

Both. Live sessions are scheduled weekly; recordings are available in the LMS within 24 hours.
