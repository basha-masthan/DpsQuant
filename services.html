Our Services

Comprehensive training solutions to help students and professionals enhance their skills and achieve career success.

CRT Training

Specialized training to prepare students for campus recruitment drives, including Technical, Aptitude, Verbal Ability, and Soft Skills. We help you get ready for every step of the recruitment process.

What You'll Learn:

  • Technical Training: Programming languages (C, Java, Python), Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Aptitude Training: Logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude, verbal ability
  • Soft Skills: Communication, leadership, teamwork, time management
  • Mock Interviews: Real-time interview practice with industry experts

Duration:

3-6 months intensive training program with flexible scheduling options.

Academic Training

We provide training programs designed to strengthen your academic foundation. These programs focus on core subjects and help students perform better in exams and assignments.

Core Subjects Covered:

  • Mathematics: Calculus, Discrete Mathematics, Statistics
  • Computer Science: Programming Fundamentals, Database Management
  • Engineering Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Electronics
  • Study Techniques: Time management, exam strategies, problem-solving methods

Benefits:

Improve grades, build strong conceptual understanding, and develop analytical thinking skills essential for higher education and career success.

  • UI/UX Design: Figma, Adobe XD, User Research, Prototyping

Hands-on projects, real-world case studies, and industry-standard tools to ensure practical skill development.
