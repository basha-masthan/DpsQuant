Our CRT Model

Our Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) is designed to prepare students for every stage of the recruitment process, from technical interviews to soft skills assessments. We offer a structured approach that ensures students are job-ready with the right skills and confidence.

The CRT Model Breakdown

Technical Training

We cover core technical concepts that are commonly tested in interviews. The focus is on programming, problem-solving, and data structures.

Aptitude Training

This section helps students improve their analytical skills, covering quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and speed math.

Verbal Ability Training

Students learn the language skills needed for interviews, focusing on grammar, vocabulary, and reading comprehension.

Soft Skills Development

We focus on building confidence, communication, teamwork, and leadership qualities that make a difference in any interview.

How Our CRT Model Works

1. Assessment

We start with an initial assessment to understand each student’s strengths and areas for improvement.

2. Personalized Learning

Based on the assessment, we provide personalized training and resources, focusing on weak points.

3. Practice & Mock Sessions

Regular practice through mock tests, mock interviews, and real-life scenarios to simulate the recruitment process.
