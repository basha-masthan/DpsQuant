Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our training programs and services.

What makes DpsQuant different from other training institutes?

+

At DpsQuant, we focus on industry-relevant skills and practical training. Our trainers are experienced professionals who bring real-world insights, and our programs are designed to make students job-ready rather than just academically prepared.

Do you provide placement assistance?

+

Yes, we offer comprehensive placement assistance including resume building, interview preparation, mock interviews, and connections with our partner companies. We have a strong track record of helping students secure jobs in top companies.

What programming languages do you teach?

+

We teach industry-relevant programming languages including C, Java, Python, and web development technologies. Our curriculum is regularly updated to match current industry demands and trends.

Are your certifications recognized by companies?

+

Yes, our certifications are recognized by leading companies. We partner with industry leaders and our training programs are designed to meet industry standards, making our graduates highly employable.

Do you offer online training options?

+

We offer both classroom and online training options. Our online programs include live interactive sessions, recorded lectures, and hands-on projects to ensure the same quality of education as our in-person classes.

What is the duration of your training programs?

+

Our program durations vary based on the course and intensity. We offer short-term workshops (1-2 weeks), semester-long programs (3-6 months), and comprehensive year-long courses. We also provide flexible scheduling options.
