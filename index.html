Frequently Asked Questions

What is Campus Recruitment Training (CRT)?

Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) is a specialized program designed to prepare pre-final and final year college students for the entire campus placement process. It covers technical skills, aptitude (quantitative and logical reasoning), verbal ability, and soft skills (like resume writing, group discussions, and interview techniques).

How many students have you placed?

We have a strong and proven track record of success. In the last 90 days alone, we are proud to have placed over 500 students in various IT and core sectors. Our focus on hands-on knowledge and industry-level training is the key to this success.

Are your trainers experienced?

Absolutely. Our trainers are not just academics; they are industry professionals with years of real-world experience. They bring practical insights and up-to-date knowledge from the field, ensuring you learn what companies are actually looking for.

Do I get a certification after completing a course?

Yes, upon successful completion of our programs, you will receive a certification from DpsQuant Pvt Ltd. These certifications are recognized by our network of partner companies and add significant value to your resume.
